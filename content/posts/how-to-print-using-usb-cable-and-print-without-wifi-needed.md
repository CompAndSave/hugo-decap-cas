Need to print something, but your printer is not connecting to Wi-Fi? You do not have to spend time troubleshooting your wireless network just to print a document. Learning how to print using USB cable gives you a simple way to connect your printer directly to your computer and start [printing without Wi-Fi](https://www.compandsave.com/blog/posts/how-to-print-without-wifi-8-easy-ways-to-print-from-any-device.html).

A USB connection can also be useful when your wireless printer keeps disconnecting, your internet is down, or you simply want a more direct connection. In this guide, you will learn how printing using USB cable works, which cable you need, how to connect your printer, and what to do if your computer does not recognize it.

## **Can You Print Using a USB Cable Without Wi-Fi?**

Yes, you can print using a USB cable without Wi-Fi. A direct USB connection allows your computer to communicate with the printer without requiring a wireless network or internet connection.

This can be especially helpful when you need to print something quickly and your Wi-Fi is unavailable. As long as your printer supports USB printing, you usually only need the correct cable, a computer, printer drivers, and paper and ink.

With a USB connection:

* Your computer sends the print job directly to the printer.
* Wi-Fi is not required.
* Internet access is not required for normal printing.
* You can print documents, photos, and other supported files.
* The connection is usually simple to set up.

Keep in mind that some printers are designed primarily for wireless printing, so check your printer's specifications before buying a cable.

## **What Is the Printer Cable Called?**

The cable commonly used to connect a printer to a computer is called a USB printer cable. Many traditional printers use a USB Type-A connector on the computer end and a USB Type-B connector on the printer end.

The USB Type-B end has a distinctive square shape that plugs into the printer. The Type-A end is the familiar rectangular USB connector that traditionally plugs into a computer.

However, newer computers may have USB-C ports instead of traditional USB-A ports. If your computer does not have a compatible port, you may need a USB-C cable or an appropriate adapter.

Before purchasing a cable, check both devices. Look at the USB port on your printer and the available port on your computer to make sure the cable will fit both.

## **What Do You Need to Print With a USB Cable?**

You do not need a complicated setup to print through USB. Most home printers can be connected with a few basic items.

You will generally need:

1. **A printer with a USB port:** Check the back or side of the printer for the appropriate port.
2. **A compatible USB cable:** Make sure both ends match your printer and computer.
3. **A computer:** Your Windows PC or Mac will send the print jobs to the printer.
4. **Printer drivers or software:** Your computer may install these automatically, but some printers require manual installation.
5. **Paper and ink:** Make sure your printer has enough supplies before starting.

If you already have these items, you can usually complete the connection in just a few minutes.

## **How Do You Connect a Printer to a Computer With USB?**

Connecting a printer with USB is generally straightforward. You simply connect the cable between the two devices, turn the printer on, and allow your computer to recognize the printer.

Follow these steps:

### **1. Turn on your printer**

Start by plugging your printer into a power outlet and turning it on. Wait until the printer finishes its startup process.

If your printer displays an error message, resolve the issue before trying to connect it to your computer.

### **2. Find the USB port**

Look at the back or side of the printer for the USB port. It is often labeled USB or marked with the standard USB symbol.

Do not force the connector into the port. USB connectors are designed to fit in a specific orientation.

### **3. Connect the USB cable**

Plug the printer end of the cable into the printer. Connect the other end to an available USB port on your computer.

Once connected, your computer may display a notification that it has detected a new device.

### **4. Wait for the printer to install**

Windows or macOS may automatically detect the printer and install the necessary software.

If the printer is not detected automatically, you may need to download and install the appropriate printer driver from the manufacturer's website.

### **5. Check your printer settings**

After installation, open your computer's printer settings and look for your connected printer.

If you see the printer listed as an available device, the USB connection is likely working correctly.

### **6. Print a test document**

Open a document or photo and select Print. Choose your USB-connected printer from the list and send the job.

If the page prints correctly, your setup is complete.

## **How Do You Print via USB on Windows?**

Windows can usually recognize a USB-connected printer automatically, but the exact setup can vary depending on your Windows version and printer model. If the printer does not appear, adding it through Windows printer settings can help.

Here’s how to print via USB on a Windows computer:

1. Connect the printer to your computer with the USB cable.
2. Turn on the printer.
3. Open Settings on your Windows computer.
4. Go to Bluetooth & devices and select Printers & scanners.
5. Look for your printer in the list.
6. If necessary, choose the option to add a printer or scanner.
7. Select your printer when it appears.
8. Follow the on-screen instructions.
9. Open your document and select Print.
10. Choose the connected printer and confirm the print job.

If Windows does not detect the printer, unplug the USB cable and reconnect it. You can also try another USB port on your computer.

## **How Can You Print From a Mac Using USB?**

Mac users can also connect many printers directly using USB. After connecting the printer, macOS may automatically identify and configure it.

Here’s how to print using USB cable with your Mac:

1. Connect the printer to your Mac using the appropriate USB cable.
2. Turn on the printer.
3. Open System Settings.
4. Select Printers & Scanners.
5. Check whether your printer appears.
6. If needed, select the option to add a printer.
7. Choose your printer from the available devices.
8. Open the document you want to print.
9. Select File, then Print.
10. Choose your printer and start the print job.

If your Mac does not recognize the printer, check whether your printer model is compatible with your macOS version. Installing updated printer software may also resolve the problem.

## **Why Is My Printer Not Printing Through USB?**

If your printer not printing through USB, the problem may not be the printer itself. A faulty cable, incorrect driver, damaged USB port, offline printer status, or stuck print queue can prevent a print job from reaching the printer.

Try these solutions in order:

* **Check the USB cable:** Make sure both ends are securely connected. If possible, test another compatible cable.
* **Try another USB port:** The computer's USB port may be the problem. Connect the cable to another available port.
* **Restart both devices:** Turn off the printer and computer. Wait a few seconds, then turn them back on and reconnect the printer.
* **Check the printer status:** Make sure the printer is not showing an error, such as a paper jam, empty cartridge, or offline status.
* **Check the selected printer:** Your computer may be trying to send the job to another printer. Open the print settings and make sure your USB-connected printer is selected.
* **Clear stuck print jobs:** A previous print job can sometimes prevent new documents from printing. Open your computer's print queue and cancel jobs that are stuck.
* **Update or reinstall the driver:** An outdated or incorrect driver can stop your computer from communicating properly with the printer.

These steps can solve many common USB printing problems without requiring professional repair.

## **Is Printing Using USB Cable Better Than Wi-Fi?**

There is no single best connection for everyone. USB is often a good choice when you want a direct and dependable connection between one computer and one printer.

Printing using USB cable can be useful when:

* Your Wi-Fi connection is unreliable.
* Your internet service is temporarily unavailable.
* You want a simple printer setup.
* You primarily print from one computer.
* You do not want to troubleshoot wireless settings.

Wireless printing can be more convenient when multiple people need to access the same printer from different devices. You can also print from smartphones, tablets, and laptops without physically connecting each device.

The right choice depends on how you use your printer. If reliability and simplicity are your priorities, USB is an excellent option.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

### **Can I print without Wi-Fi using a USB cable?**

Yes, you can print without Wi-Fi when your printer is connected directly to your computer with a USB cable. The computer sends the print job directly to the printer. You only need Wi-Fi if a specific printer feature or software requires an internet connection.

### **What cable do I need to connect my printer to my computer?**

Many printers use a USB Type-A to USB Type-B cable for connecting to traditional computers. Newer computers may require a USB-C connection or an adapter. Check the ports on both your printer and computer before buying a replacement cable.

### **Why does my computer not recognize my USB printer?**

Your computer may not recognize the printer because of a loose connection, faulty USB cable, incompatible port, or missing printer driver. Try another USB port and restart both the computer and printer. If that does not work, install the latest compatible driver for your printer.

### **Is USB printing faster than wireless printing?**

USB printing can provide a direct and stable connection because the print data travels straight from your computer to the printer. However, actual printing speed depends on your printer model, document size, and other factors. For everyday documents, the difference may not be noticeable.
