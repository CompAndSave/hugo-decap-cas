When your printer not feeding paper interrupts an important task, even a simple print job can become frustrating. It can stop you from printing work, school assignments, or business documents. You can often solve the problem with a few careful checks.

Paper-feed problems usually come from a tray that isn’t loaded correctly, unsuitable paper, a blockage, or rollers that can’t grip the sheets. Start with the simplest fixes and test your printer after each step. That way, you can find the cause of a printer not grabbing paper without making unnecessary changes.

## **Why Isn’t Your Printer Feeding Paper?**

Your printer may not feed paper because the sheets are misaligned, damp, or unsuitable for the printer. A tray that isn’t seated properly, a blockage, or dirty pickup rollers can also prevent the printer from gripping a page.

Notice what happens when you send a print job. Does the printer make a feeding sound but pick up nothing? Does it pull in several sheets at once? Or does an error appear even though paper is loaded? These clues can help you choose where to start.

Common causes include:

* **Misaligned paper:** The stack may sit unevenly or press too tightly against the tray guides.
* **Damaged or unsuitable sheets:** Curled, torn, damp, or overly thick paper can be difficult to feed.
* **An overfilled tray:** Too many sheets can keep the printer from separating and picking up one page.
* **Dirty or worn rollers:** Dust and residue can make the rollers slippery.
* **A [paper jam](https://www.compandsave.com/blog/posts/how-to-fix-paper-jam-in-printer-reasons-and-solutions.html) or obstruction:** A small scrap of paper can block the feed path.
* **Incorrect settings:** The print job may use a different paper size or source than the one you’ve loaded.

These causes can overlap, so work through the checks one at a time. Start with the paper and tray, then inspect for a jam or roller problem if the printer still won’t feed.

## **How Should You Check and Reload the Paper?**

Remove the paper, check each sheet, and reload a neat stack that fits the tray. Adjust the guides so they touch the paper’s edges without bending or squeezing the sheets.

Cancel the print job and take the paper out of the tray. Fan the stack gently to separate sheets that may be sticking together, then tap the edges on a flat surface to align them. Check the tray markings or your printer’s manual for the correct size and orientation.

Look for bent, curled, torn, damp, or stuck-together sheets. Replace any damaged paper with fresh, dry sheets. If you’ve stored the paper somewhere humid, try a new stack, and avoid mixing different paper types or sizes in the tray.

Reload the paper without exceeding the tray’s fill line. Slide the guides against the edges, but don’t push them so tightly that the stack bows. Make sure the tray is fully inserted before you try printing a single page.

If your printer is not taking paper after these steps, move on to the tray and print settings.

## **Could the Paper Tray or Print Settings Be the Problem?**

Yes. A tray that isn’t seated correctly or settings that don’t match your paper can prevent a job from feeding. Check that the tray is fully inserted and that the selected paper size and source are correct.

Pull out the tray and look for scraps, loose labels, or anything that could prevent it from moving freely. Reinsert it straight and firmly. If your printer has multiple paper sources, such as a rear feed slot, check that the job is set to use the source where you’ve loaded paper.

Next, open the print settings on your computer or phone. The selected size, such as Letter or A4, should match the paper in the tray. If you see a paper-source option, choose the correct tray. Try printing a test page to check whether the problem affects every job or only one document.

If you’re unsure which settings to use, consult your printer’s manual or support page. Change one setting at a time and test again, so it’s easier to identify what fixes the issue.

## **How Can You Clear a Paper Jam or Blockage?**

Another cause of a printer not feeding paper is a paper jam. You can fix this by turning off the printer and removing visible jammed paper slowly, following the paper path where possible. Check accessible areas for scraps, and don’t force stuck paper or reach into hot or restricted parts of the printer.

Before inspecting the printer, switch it off. If the manual recommends unplugging it before clearing a jam, follow that instruction. Open only the panels intended for user access, then check the tray area, rear access door, and output path for stuck sheets or scraps.

Use both hands to pull jammed paper steadily. If a sheet tears, look for remaining pieces because even a small scrap may block the feed. Don’t use sharp tools to reach inside or touch components the manual identifies as hot or delicate.

Once the path is clear, close the access panels, reload the paper, and run a test page. If the printer still reports a jam but you can’t see any paper, a hidden scrap or sensor problem may need further attention.

## **How Do You Clean the Paper Pickup Rollers?**

Dusty or slippery rollers can cause printer not grabbing paper problems, so careful cleaning can help restore feeding. Follow your printer’s instructions and use a soft, lint-free cloth lightly dampened with water unless the manufacturer recommends another method.

Turn off the printer and remove the paper tray. Use the manual to locate the accessible pickup rollers. Wipe the roller surface gently, rotating it only as directed so you can clean the full area. Don’t pour liquid into the printer or spray cleaner inside it.

Let the rollers dry completely before replacing the tray and turning the printer on. Load a few sheets and test the printer. If the rollers look cracked, smooth, or damaged, cleaning may not be enough. Depending on your model, replacement may require professional service.

## **What Should You Check If the Printer Still Won’t Pull Paper?**

Restart the printer and review its feed settings if you’ve checked the paper, tray, jams, and rollers but still can’t print. A continuing problem may point to a worn part, sensor issue, or model-specific fault.

Cancel any stuck jobs in the print queue, then restart the printer and the device sending the job. Look for error messages on the printer screen or in its companion app. If your printer has a built-in paper-feed or maintenance test, run it according to the manual.

If you’re printing from a computer, confirm that you’ve selected the right printer and that its driver is current. If the problem happens from just one device or app, try sending a test page from another. If it happens with every job, the printer itself may need attention.

If your printer not pulling paper persists, avoid repeatedly resending the same job because that can lead to more jams. Contact the manufacturer or a qualified repair service if the basic checks don’t help. Have your printer model, error message, and troubleshooting steps ready.

## **How Can You Prevent Paper-Feed Problems?**

Store paper properly, load the tray carefully, and keep the feed path clear to reduce paper-feed issues. A few simple habits can help your printer pick up sheets consistently and keep you on schedule.

* Store paper flat in its original packaging in a cool, dry place.
* Use paper that matches your printer’s supported size, type, and weight.
* Keep the stack below the tray’s fill line.
* Adjust the guides so they touch the paper without squeezing it.
* Replace curled or torn sheets instead of returning them to the tray.
* Follow the manual when clearing scraps or cleaning rollers.
* Use the printer’s recommended maintenance routine.

If you don’t print often, check the paper before starting an important job. Sheets can become curled or damp during storage, and a quick inspection may prevent an unexpected interruption.

## **Conclusion**

Start by reloading fresh paper and checking that the tray guides and print settings match. If that doesn’t work, check for a jam, clean accessible rollers according to your manual, and test the printer again.

If your printer not feeding paper problem keeps returning, don’t keep resending the job without checking the cause. A few careful steps may get you printing again and help prevent future jams. If your printer not grabbing paper still won’t work after troubleshooting, contact the manufacturer or a repair technician to check for worn or damaged parts.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

### **Why Does My Printer Say There’s No Paper When the Tray Is Full?**

The tray may not be seated correctly, or the sheets may not be aligned with the guides. Remove and reload the stack below the fill line, then push the tray firmly into place. If the error continues, check for scraps in the feed path and consult your printer’s manual.

### **Why Is My Printer Picking Up More Than One Sheet at a Time?**

The sheets may be damp, stuck together, or packed too tightly in the tray. Remove the stack, fan it gently, and reload dry paper without exceeding the fill line. If the issue continues with fresh paper, a separation pad or another feed component may need service.

### **What Should You Do When Your HP Printer Isn’t Feeding Paper?**

Turn off the printer and check that the paper is supported, dry, aligned, and below the tray’s fill line. Inspect for a jam, then clean accessible rollers only as described in HP’s instructions for your model. If it still won’t feed, use HP’s model-specific troubleshooting guidance or contact support.
