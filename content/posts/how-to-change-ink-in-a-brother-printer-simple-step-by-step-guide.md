## **When Should You Replace a Brother Ink Cartridge?**

Before learning how to change a Brother ink cartridge, it's important to know when replacement is actually needed.

Most Brother printers will notify you when ink levels are low, but there are also visual signs that indicate it's time for a new cartridge:

* Your printer displays a **Replace Ink** or **Ink Low** message.
* Printed pages look faded or dull.
* Colors appear inaccurate.
* Lines or streaks appear on documents.
* The printer refuses to print because one cartridge has run out.

Replacing cartridges promptly helps prevent interruptions and maintains consistent print quality.

## **How to Change Ink in a Brother Printer: Step-by-Step**

Follow these instructions carefully to learn how to change ink in a Brother printer safely and correctly.

### **Step 1: Turn On the Printer**

Your Brother printer should be powered on before replacing ink cartridges. This allows the printer to move the cartridge holder into the correct replacement position.

### **Step 2: Open the Ink Cartridge Cover**

Open the cartridge access door located on the front of your printer.

Wait until the cartridge holder stops moving before touching anything inside.

### **Step 3: Remove the Empty Cartridge**

Press the release lever or gently push the cartridge until it unlocks.

Pull the empty cartridge straight out.

Avoid twisting or forcing the cartridge during removal.

### **Step 4: Prepare the New Cartridge**

Open the new cartridge package only when you're ready to install it.

Remove all protective packaging, plastic clips, and sealing tape according to the manufacturer's instructions.

### **Step 5: Installing Brother Ink Cartridge Correctly**

Installing Brother ink cartridges properly helps prevent common installation problems.

Match each cartridge with the correct color slot and slide it in gently until it clicks into place.

If you're replacing multiple cartridges, install each one individually before closing the printer cover.

### **Step 6: Close the Cover and Wait**

Close the cartridge access cover securely.

Your printer may take one to two minutes to recognize the newly installed cartridge before returning to Ready mode.

### **Step 7: Print a Test Page**

Once installation is complete, print a [test page](https://www.compandsave.com/blog/posts/why-you-should-print-a-test-page-regularly-compandsave.html) to verify that colors appear correctly and the printer is operating normally.

The entire process on how to change ink in a Brother printer usually takes just a few minutes and helps ensure your printer continues producing high-quality prints.

## **Common Mistakes to Avoid When Changing Brother Ink Cartridges**

Even though changing Brother ink cartridges is relatively simple, a few mistakes can lead to printing problems later.

Avoid these common errors:

* Forgetting to remove the protective seal.
* Installing the cartridge in the wrong color slot.
* Touching the electrical contacts or ink outlet.
* Forcing the cartridge into place.
* Turning off the printer during installation.
* Ignoring low-ink warnings for an extended period.

Taking a little extra care during installation can save time and prevent unnecessary troubleshooting.

## **What to Do If Your Brother Printer Doesn't Recognize the New Cartridge**

Even after learning how to change a Brother ink cartridge, your printer may occasionally fail to recognize the replacement.

Try these simple troubleshooting steps:

* Remove and reinstall the cartridge.
* Make sure all protective seals have been removed.
* Confirm you're using the correct cartridge model.
* Restart the printer.
* Clean the cartridge contacts with a lint-free cloth if necessary.

If the issue continues, consult your printer's manual or visit our guide for more detailed troubleshooting solutions.

## **Keep Your Brother Printer Performing at Its Best**

Replacing ink is only one part of maintaining your printer. A few simple maintenance habits can improve print quality and extend your printer's lifespan.

### **Cleaning Brother Printer Heads**

If your prints still appear faded or have missing lines after replacing ink, cleaning Brother printer heads can often solve the problem. Running the printer's built-in cleaning utility removes dried ink from the printhead nozzles and restores proper ink flow.

For detailed instructions and additional troubleshooting tips, check out our guide on **[cleaning Brother printer heads](https://www.compandsave.com/blog/posts/how-to-clean-printheads-on-a-brother-printer.html)**.

### **Run a Printer Nozzle Check**

After replacing ink, it's a good idea to perform a printer nozzle check. This built-in diagnostic test helps verify that every printhead nozzle is working correctly.

If the printed test pattern has gaps or missing lines, run a cleaning cycle before replacing another cartridge. Our complete **[Brother printer nozzle check](https://www.compandsave.com/blog/posts/brother-printer-nozzle-check-how-to-test-improve-print-quality.html)** guide explains how to interpret the results.

### **Print Regularly**

Printing a few pages every week helps prevent ink from drying inside the printheads and keeps your printer operating efficiently.

### **Store Spare Cartridges Properly**

Keep unopened cartridges sealed in their original packaging and store them in a cool, dry location away from direct sunlight until you're ready to use them.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

### **1. How often should I replace my Brother ink cartridge?**

Replace your cartridge whenever your printer displays a low-ink or replace-ink message, or when print quality noticeably declines. Waiting too long can interrupt printing and affect output quality.

### **2. Is installing Brother ink cartridges difficult?**

No. Installing Brother ink cartridges is designed to be simple, even for first-time users. Following the step-by-step instructions above helps ensure proper installation and reduces the chance of cartridge recognition issues.

### **3. Why is my printer still printing poorly after changing Brother ink cartridge?**

If you're changing Brother ink cartridges but still notice faded prints or missing colors, run a printhead cleaning cycle followed by a nozzle check. In many cases, clogged printheads—not the cartridge itself—are the cause of poor print quality.

### **4. Can I use a compatible ink cartridge in my Brother printer?**

Yes. A high-quality compatible ink cartridge designed specifically for your Brother printer model can provide reliable performance and excellent print quality while helping lower printing costs.

### **5. Is there a difference between how to change Brother printer ink cartridges and replacing toner?**

Yes. Changing Brother printer ink cartridge applies to Brother inkjet printers. Brother laser printers use toner cartridges, which follow a different replacement process.
