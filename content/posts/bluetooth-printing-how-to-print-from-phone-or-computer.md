When you need to print but Wi-Fi is unavailable, bluetooth printing can give you a simple alternative. It lets a compatible printer connect directly to your phone, tablet, or computer without using a Wi-Fi network.

If you want to print using bluetooth, you usually just need to pair your device with the printer and select it when sending your document or photo. This guide explains how Bluetooth printing works, how to set it up, and what to do when the connection does not work.

## **What Is Bluetooth Printing and How Does It Work?**

Bluetooth printing allows a compatible device to send a print job directly to a printer over a short-range Bluetooth connection. Unlike a typical Wi-Fi setup, the printer does not need to communicate through your home or office wireless network.

The process is similar to pairing wireless headphones with your phone. First, you make the printer discoverable, turn on Bluetooth on your device, and pair the two devices. Once they are connected, you can select the printer when sending a supported document, image, or other file.

Bluetooth can be useful when:

* You do not have access to a Wi-Fi network.
* Your Wi-Fi connection is temporarily unavailable.
* You want to print from a nearby phone or tablet.
* You are using a portable Bluetooth-compatible printer.
* You want a simple connection between one device and one printer.

Bluetooth is generally designed for short distances, so you will usually need to stay relatively close to the printer. It also may not offer the same multi-device convenience as a printer connected to your home or office network.

## **Can You Print Using Bluetooth From Any Printer?**

No. You cannot automatically use Bluetooth with every printer because the printer must have Bluetooth hardware and support the appropriate Bluetooth printing features.

A printer described as "wireless" does not necessarily support Bluetooth. Some wireless printers use Wi-Fi only, while others support Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, USB, or several connection methods.

Before trying to pair your printer, check:

* **The printer specifications:** Look for Bluetooth connectivity in the product specifications.
* **The printer settings:** Some models have a Bluetooth option that needs to be enabled.
* **The user manual:** The manual should explain whether Bluetooth pairing is supported.
* **The manufacturer's app:** Some printers require a companion mobile app to complete the setup.
* **Your device compatibility:** Your phone, tablet, or computer must also support the required connection.

Checking compatibility first can save you from spending time trying to pair a printer that was never designed to work over Bluetooth.

## **What Devices Can You Use for Bluetooth Printing?**

You may be able to print through Bluetooth from a smartphone, tablet, laptop, or computer, depending on the printer and operating system. The exact steps can vary between Android, iPhone, iPad, Windows, and other devices.

### **Android Phones and Tablets**

Many Android devices include Bluetooth and can connect to compatible printers. Depending on the printer, you may need the manufacturer's printing app or an Android-compatible printing service.

### **iPhones and iPads**

Apple devices support several [wireless printing](https://www.compandsave.com/blog/posts/wired-printing-vs-wireless-printing-which-is-better.html) methods, but Bluetooth printing is not universally supported in the same way it is on every Android device. Many printers designed for Apple devices rely on Wi-Fi and AirPrint instead.

If you have an iPhone or iPad, check the printer manufacturer's compatibility information before assuming Bluetooth will work.

### **Windows Laptops and Computers**

Windows devices can support Bluetooth connections, but the printer may still require a compatible driver or manufacturer software. Pairing the printer through Windows Bluetooth settings does not always mean the printer is ready to accept print jobs.

The key is to confirm that your specific printer supports Bluetooth printing with your operating system.

## **How Do You Connect a Printer via Bluetooth?**

To connect printer via bluetooth, both the printer and your device need to have Bluetooth enabled and be close enough to communicate. The exact menu names can vary by printer and device, but the general process is similar.

### **Step 1: Turn on the printer**

Make sure the printer is powered on and ready. If your printer has a Bluetooth button or setting, enable it according to the manufacturer's instructions.

### **Step 2: Turn on Bluetooth on your device**

Open your phone, tablet, or computer's Bluetooth settings and switch Bluetooth on. Keep this screen open while you look for available devices.

### **Step 3: Put the printer into pairing mode**

Some printers automatically become discoverable when Bluetooth is enabled. Others require you to press a Bluetooth button or select a pairing option from the printer's control panel.

### **Step 4: Select the printer**

Look through the available Bluetooth devices on your phone, tablet, or computer. Select the printer when it appears.

### **Step 5: Confirm the pairing**

Your device may ask you to confirm a pairing request or enter a PIN. Follow the instructions shown on your screen or in the printer manual.

Once the printer is paired, you can move to the printing process.

## **How to Print by Bluetooth From Your Device?**

Once your printer is paired successfully, how to print by bluetooth will depend on the device, app, and printer model. The general process is to open your file, choose the print option, select the paired printer, and send the document.

Try these steps:

1. Open the document, image, email, or webpage you want to print.
2. Select **Share**, **Menu**, or **Print**, depending on the app.
3. Choose the available print option.
4. Select your paired Bluetooth printer if it appears.
5. Check the number of copies, paper size, orientation, and other settings.
6. Tap or click **Print**.

Before sending an important document, check that the correct printer is selected. You should also make sure there is enough paper and ink or toner to complete the job.

If the printer does not appear in the print menu even though it is paired, the printer may require a dedicated app or printing service. Pairing and printing are related but are not always the same process.

## **Is Bluetooth Printing Better Than Wi-Fi Printing?**

Bluetooth and Wi-Fi printing serve different purposes, so one is not automatically better than the other. Bluetooth can be convenient for short-range printing from a nearby device, while Wi-Fi is often more practical when several devices need access to the same printer.

Bluetooth may be a better fit when you:

* Need to [print without a Wi-Fi](https://www.compandsave.com/blog/posts/how-to-print-without-wifi-8-easy-ways-to-print-from-any-device.html) network.
* Are printing from one nearby device.
* Want a simple direct connection.
* Use a portable printer.
* Do not need multiple people to access the printer.

Wi-Fi may be more convenient when you:

* Print from several phones or computers.
* Share a printer with family members or coworkers.
* Need to print from different rooms.
* Regularly send multiple print jobs.
* Want broader network access to your printer.

Bluetooth can be especially useful when you are traveling, working in a location without a reliable network, or using a compact portable printer.

Remember that neither method necessarily requires internet access for local printing. A printer connected to your Wi-Fi network can often receive print jobs even when the internet itself is unavailable.

## **Why Is My Printer Not Showing Up on Bluetooth?**

If your printer does not appear in your device's Bluetooth list, start by checking whether the printer actually supports Bluetooth. A wireless connection option does not necessarily mean Bluetooth is available.

If compatibility is confirmed, try these troubleshooting steps:

* **Check Bluetooth:** Make sure Bluetooth is enabled on both devices.
* **Check pairing mode:** Your printer may need to be placed into discoverable mode.
* **Move closer:** Bluetooth works over a limited range, so keep the device near the printer.
* **Restart both devices:** Turn the printer and your phone, tablet, or computer off and back on.
* **Remove an old pairing:** If the printer was previously connected to another device, remove the old connection if necessary.
* **Check for another active connection:** Some printers may have limitations when already connected to another device.
* **Review the manual:** Your printer may use a specific pairing process.

If the printer still does not appear, check the manufacturer's support information. The problem may be a compatibility limitation rather than a hardware failure.

## **Final Thoughts**

Bluetooth printing can give you a convenient way to print when Wi-Fi is unavailable or you simply want a direct connection between your device and printer. If you want to print using bluetooth, start by confirming that your printer supports Bluetooth, pair the correct devices, and then use the appropriate print option or manufacturer app.

When Bluetooth does not work, check compatibility, pairing mode, device range, printer status, and software before assuming the printer is broken. Keeping enough paper and ink on hand also helps prevent connection problems from turning into failed print jobs.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

### **Can I print from my phone using Bluetooth?**

Yes, you can print from some phones using Bluetooth if both the phone and printer support Bluetooth printing. You may need to pair the devices first and use a compatible printing app. Always check your printer's specifications because Bluetooth printing support varies between models.

### **Can I print without Wi-Fi using Bluetooth?**

Yes, a compatible Bluetooth printer can allow you to print without connecting the printer to a Wi-Fi network. Your device and printer communicate directly through Bluetooth instead. Keep in mind that both devices must support Bluetooth printing for this method to work.

### **Why can't my phone find my Bluetooth printer?**

Your phone may not find the printer if Bluetooth is disabled, the printer is not in pairing mode, or the printer does not support Bluetooth printing. Move your phone closer to the printer and restart Bluetooth on both devices. If the printer still does not appear, check the manufacturer's pairing instructions and compatibility information.
